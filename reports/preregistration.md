## Outcomes and decision rule

The primary estimand is the mean paired difference in exact complete-trace pass@1 between a solver trained with three frozen judges and one trained with two. Evaluate each final checkpoint once by greedy decoding on the same 2,048 unique in-distribution problem IDs. Average the paired differences across seeds 17, 29 and 43 with equal weight. Resample whole problem IDs with replacement 10,000 times, preserving arms, seeds and all variants within a problem. Report a percentile 95% interval conditional on these checkpoints, per-seed estimates and spread. A lower endpoint above +0.03 supports the practical target. An upper endpoint below +0.03 rules out a gain of that size. All other outcomes are inconclusive for that target. Missing pairs or seeds preclude the registered pooled decision. The week is a compute target, not a significance stopping rule.

Secondary outcomes include D3 versus three independent J1 votes, each solver's final-answer-only accuracy, and the change in exact reward classification from D2 to D3 on identical natural traces. Report the two directional transition counts, false accepts, false rejects and malformed reports with their denominators. A judge's approval of a sound rejection never earns a positive solver reward.

On 128 distinct mechanism problems, keep the problem, solution, J1 report and all J2 decisions fixed. Change only J2's numerical supporting evidence. Require J3 to accept the sound report and reject the altered one with the correct field, correction and effective solution verdict. Compare whole-pair accuracy against the same J3 with the preceding evidence fields masked in both twins. Score the masked outputs against the original labels. Use identical deterministic decoding. Keep constructed twins and natural reviews in separate summaries. No confirmatory multiplicity-adjusted inference is planned for secondary outcomes.

## Population and separation

Use signed integers and the operators +, − and ×. Present operations in postorder, with references to earlier numbered steps. In-distribution problems have two to four operations. Exclude trees with internal nodes on both sides of the root from this population. Evaluate those topologies separately, and evaluate long problems with five to seven operations separately. Allocate 128 distinct problems to each transfer stratum. Reserve a magnitude-changing sign reversal of numerical evidence for the transfer diagnostic. Record test split identities by a canonical expression hash that normalizes commutative operand order. Assign the split before constructing any answer, report, or mutation. Use distinct deterministic generator streams and deduplicate by identity. Test data never enter judge training, prompts as exemplars, checkpoint selection, or the live development display.

The outputs use comma-separated integers. Solver output lists the requested values. J1 returns the solution verdict, first wrong step (zero for a valid trace), and exact corrected value (root value for a valid trace). A malformed solution has ground truth (0,-1,0). J2 and J3 prepend the prior-report soundness bit, first incorrect field (one-based), and its replacement. A sound report uses field zero and repeats the supported value. A malformed predecessor uses field minus one and replacement zero. Every upper report also includes the full corrected J1 triple. A syntactically malformed terminal judge yields zero reward and a counted failure; a later judge may repair a lower report. Format-only success earns zero.

Use TRL's GRPO loss, group size four, learning rate 0.00003, clipping 0.2, KL coefficient zero, one policy iteration and group-scaled rewards. Each optimizer update consumes one prompt and four completions, with microbatch one. Use temperature 0.8, full sampling support and a common ceiling of 48 new tokens for solver and judge calls. Use deterministic evaluation for the solver and mechanism twins. Independent votes and the recursive reward calls use sampled judgments. All compared training runs share initialization and problem order within seed. Independent judge RNG streams prevent the judge depth from changing future solver sampling.
