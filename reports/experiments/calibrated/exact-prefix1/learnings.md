# Observations from calibrated/exact-prefix1

The exact checker accepted 43.6% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 72.1%. Of 1024 rollouts, 411 received reward for an incorrect output and 119 lost reward for a correct output. 96 of 256 complete groups had identical rewards.
