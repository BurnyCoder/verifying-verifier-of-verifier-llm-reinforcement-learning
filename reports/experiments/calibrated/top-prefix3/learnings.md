# Observations from calibrated/top-prefix3

The exact checker accepted 74.9% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 93.2%. Of 1024 rollouts, 232 received reward for an incorrect output and 45 lost reward for a correct output. 197 of 256 complete groups had identical rewards.
