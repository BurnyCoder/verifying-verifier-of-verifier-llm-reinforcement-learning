# Observations from calibrated/top-prefix2

The exact checker accepted 75.7% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 83.4%. Of 1024 rollouts, 184 received reward for an incorrect output and 105 lost reward for a correct output. 137 of 256 complete groups had identical rewards.
