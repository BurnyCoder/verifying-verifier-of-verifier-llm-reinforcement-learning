# Observations from calibrated/d3-17

The exact checker accepted 14.1% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 64.2%. Of 1024 rollouts, 569 received reward for an incorrect output and 56 lost reward for a correct output. 78 of 256 complete groups had identical rewards.
