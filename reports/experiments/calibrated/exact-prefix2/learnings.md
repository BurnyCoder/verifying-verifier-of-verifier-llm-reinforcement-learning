# Observations from calibrated/exact-prefix2

The exact checker accepted 11.7% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 88.7%. Of 1024 rollouts, 803 received reward for an incorrect output and 15 lost reward for a correct output. 174 of 256 complete groups had identical rewards.
