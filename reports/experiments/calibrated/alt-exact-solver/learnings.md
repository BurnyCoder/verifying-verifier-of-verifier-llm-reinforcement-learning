# Observations from calibrated/alt-exact-solver

The exact checker accepted 38.1% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 99.8%. Of 1024 rollouts, 633 received reward for an incorrect output and 1 lost reward for a correct output. 254 of 256 complete groups had identical rewards.
