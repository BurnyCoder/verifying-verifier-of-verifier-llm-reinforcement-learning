# Observations from calibrated/shuffle-17

The exact checker accepted 40.4% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 65.7%. Of 1024 rollouts, 394 received reward for an incorrect output and 135 lost reward for a correct output. 79 of 256 complete groups had identical rewards.
