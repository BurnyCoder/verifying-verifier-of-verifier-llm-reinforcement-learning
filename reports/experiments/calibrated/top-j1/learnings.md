# Observations from calibrated/top-j1

The exact checker accepted 37.0% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 99.0%. Of 1024 rollouts, 637 received reward for an incorrect output and 2 lost reward for a correct output. 246 of 256 complete groups had identical rewards.
