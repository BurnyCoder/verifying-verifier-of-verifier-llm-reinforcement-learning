# Observations from calibrated/top-depth2

The exact checker accepted 50.0% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 96.2%. Of 80 rollouts, 38 received reward for an incorrect output and 1 lost reward for a correct output. 17 of 20 complete groups had identical rewards.
