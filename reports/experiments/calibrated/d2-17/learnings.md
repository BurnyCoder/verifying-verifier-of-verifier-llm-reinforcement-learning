# Observations from calibrated/d2-17

The exact checker accepted 63.3% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 73.3%. Of 1024 rollouts, 261 received reward for an incorrect output and 158 lost reward for a correct output. 96 of 256 complete groups had identical rewards.
