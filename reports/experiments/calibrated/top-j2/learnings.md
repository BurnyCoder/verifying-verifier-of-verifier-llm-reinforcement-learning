# Observations from calibrated/top-j2

The exact checker accepted 25.8% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 98.7%. Of 1024 rollouts, 747 received reward for an incorrect output and 0 lost reward for a correct output. 246 of 256 complete groups had identical rewards.
