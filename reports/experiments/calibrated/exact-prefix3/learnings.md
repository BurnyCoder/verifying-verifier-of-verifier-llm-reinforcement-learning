# Observations from calibrated/exact-prefix3

The exact checker accepted 63.7% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 99.9%. Of 1024 rollouts, 371 received reward for an incorrect output and 0 lost reward for a correct output. 255 of 256 complete groups had identical rewards.
