# Observations from calibrated/d1-17

The exact checker accepted 46.0% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 94.2%. Of 1024 rollouts, 510 received reward for an incorrect output and 16 lost reward for a correct output. 218 of 256 complete groups had identical rewards.
