# Observations from calibrated/flat-17

The exact checker accepted 77.6% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 97.0%. Of 1024 rollouts, 210 received reward for an incorrect output and 12 lost reward for a correct output. 240 of 256 complete groups had identical rewards.
