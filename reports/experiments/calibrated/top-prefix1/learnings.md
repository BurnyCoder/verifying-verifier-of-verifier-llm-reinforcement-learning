# Observations from calibrated/top-prefix1

The exact checker accepted 22.9% of observed policy outputs. The assigned reward averaged 70.3%. Of 1024 rollouts, 550 received reward for an incorrect output and 64 lost reward for a correct output. 83 of 256 complete groups had identical rewards.
