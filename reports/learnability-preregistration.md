## Question and precedent

Can the same frozen Qwen model produce enough correct and incorrect completions of the *same* problem for an informative GRPO update? Does training then improve complete-trace accuracy on fresh problems? DAPO filters groups with uniform rewards. CurES studies intermediate difficulty and adaptive sampling, including experiments with a math-specialized 1.5B model. We use these observations to calibrate a fixed population before comparing reward sources. No study training group will be dropped based on its observed reward. Under four independent Bernoulli draws with success probability p, the probability of a mixed group is 1 − p⁴ − (1 − p)⁴. We measure mixed groups directly because difficulty varies by problem. ## Frozen-model screen

Cross four arithmetic families with two prompt styles. The families are one nonnegative-operand addition or subtraction, two successive additions/subtractions, two small mixed operations including multiplication, and the original two-to-four-step signed arithmetic distribution. The styles are the original instructions and completed user/assistant demonstrations of the exact output syntax. All styles retain the strict integer parser, first-error evidence semantics, frozen backbone, temperature, token ceiling and four-completion groups. A demonstration uses only published example identities excluded from the study.

On sixteen unique screening problems per family, sample four solver completions and score one greedy completion. Within a family, prompt styles see the same problems. Eligible configurations have at least 75% parseable samples, sampled exact accuracy from 15% to 85%, greedy exact accuracy from 10% to 90%, and at least two mixed groups. Choose the configuration closest to 50% sampled exact accuracy, then higher mixed-group fraction, higher parse rate, then fixed family/style order. No judge result enters selection. On forty-eight new confirmation problems require at least 60% parseability, sampled accuracy from 5% to 95%, and at least one mixed group. If no configuration qualifies or confirmation fails, report the floor or ceiling and register a revised diagnostic before using fresh data.

Keep all calibration data in the original training hash partition. Exclude all problems from the original solver's registered training pool and all worked examples. A second salted hash assigns fixed, disjoint screening, confirmation, optimization, learning-validation and future benchmark partitions across arithmetic families and prompt styles. No final benchmark labels enter calibration.

## Does optimization teach the solver anything?

Fit only the solver, using the original optimizer and a fresh seed-17 adapter. Train one exact-checker positive control and one control with the same exact rewards randomly permuted within each four-completion group. Each receives 128 optimizer updates on the same ordered training prompts. The original three-frozen-judge solver was started first. These shorter diagnostic arms test whether the arithmetic population supplies a usable learning signal before the remaining long comparisons.

Compare the untrained, exact-trained and shuffled-control checkpoints on the same 256 fresh learning-validation problems. Use one greedy completion per problem. Count every intermediate value and format error. Report paired percentage-point differences and percentile 95% intervals from 10,000 resamples of problem IDs. Call learning demonstrated for this seed if the exact-trained checkpoint gains at least five points over the starting model with a lower endpoint above zero. Attribute a gain specifically to informative reward only if the exact-minus-shuffled interval also lies above zero. This single planned endpoint is developmental evidence conditional on these checkpoints. Training reward, parse rate and high initial accuracy alone cannot meet this criterion.

On the confirmed family, separately replay matched correct and single-error solutions from sixteen problems through the frozen three-judge chain. Report each judge’s sensitivity and specificity, balanced accuracy, malformed reports and whole-evidence correctness. Run the evidence-altered Judge 2 twins with Judge 3.
