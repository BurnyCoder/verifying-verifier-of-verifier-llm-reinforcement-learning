The next row uses the solver trained with three. After training, both sets of saved answers went through all three judges. Each judge column translates its claims into plain language, with the original output underneath.

Judge 1 writes three numbers. The first is its verdict (1 means accept, 0 means reject). The second is the first wrong step (0 means none). The third is the final value it certifies on acceptance or the correct value at the first faulty step on rejection. Judges 2 and 3 first return whether the preceding report was sound, the first faulty field and its replacement. Their last three fields grade the solver. Invalid required verdict flags or unparseable reports yield an effective rejection. The independent integer checker determines the correct or wrong label in the solver column.
