# Review of the independent frozen-judge confirmation

The audit regenerated the 64 assigned confirmation problem IDs. None overlapped the 64 distinct development problems observed during screening, including the natural traces. The original judge and selected file received identical inputs on all 192 matched cases, including the two versions of Judge 2's evidence. Each problem supplied a valid trace, an invalid trace and one evidence-pair case. [Control records](https://github.com/BurnyCoder/verifying-verifier-of-verifier-llm-reinforcement-learning/tree/main/results/new-judges/confirm-control) · [Selected model records](https://github.com/BurnyCoder/verifying-verifier-of-verifier-llm-reinforcement-learning/tree/main/results/new-judges/confirm-winner).

The release recomputed the rank of 3 completed candidates, verified that the two confirmation configurations matched their screen files and runtime settings, and regenerated both registered bootstrap intervals and the online eligibility decision. The winning screen record was `q35-4b`. [Selection](https://github.com/BurnyCoder/verifying-verifier-of-verifier-llm-reinforcement-learning/tree/main/results/new-judges/decision.json) · [Reconstruction](https://github.com/BurnyCoder/verifying-verifier-of-verifier-llm-reinforcement-learning/blob/main/src/study/frozen_audit.py).

The intervals condition on these frozen deployments and the arithmetic generator. Model size, post-training and compression vary together. The sustained-throughput fallback was declared after partial inference at higher precision. A trained solver's response to these rewards remains a separate experiment.
