# State of the art and implementation choices

The closest comparator for the architecture is Wen et al.'s third-order pairwise critique. Their AI–AI downstream experiment trained a reward model and selected responses by best-of-N. The closest comparator for the evidence objective is DeepSeekMath-V2, which used experts' assessments to train a proof-analysis meta-verifier. This experiment asks a narrower causal question about a compact pointwise review becoming an online solver reward. The prior studies use different tasks, model sizes and budgets, so their published scores cannot enter our evaluation table. [Wen et al., §5](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.04675v4) · [DeepSeekMath-V2, §2](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22570v1)

The fixed base is the [Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct revision](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct/tree/989aa7980e4cf806f80c7fef2b1adb7bc71aa306). All roles start from the same weights. This controls model identity while allowing correlated errors. The experiment estimates the effect for this shared-model system. Structured numerical evidence makes faults observable at the cost of limiting conclusions about unrestricted natural-language criticisms. [ProcessBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06559v1)

Use the maintained [TRL GRPO trainer](https://huggingface.co/docs/trl/grpo_trainer) for grouped sampling and policy updates. Pin its loss to GRPO. Use the [PEFT 4-bit preparation procedure](https://huggingface.co/docs/peft/developer_guides/quantization) and role-specific low-rank adapters to fit the local accelerator. Reasoning Gym's arithmetic generator supplies the expression-sampling design. Restrict its operators to addition, subtraction and multiplication so every witness has exact integer semantics. [Pinned upstream generator](https://github.com/open-thought/reasoning-gym/blob/5aea50d292a6427f12c63c77873ec8ae945cd2c0/reasoning_gym/arithmetic/basic_arithmetic.py)

An untrained policy, a shallow judge, flat voting, an exact checker, a hybrid checker, and shuffled group rewards address different explanations for a gain. Only the exact-checker arm gives the solver an entirely programmatic RLVR signal. The other arms disclose the learned component of their reward. [Verifier robustness](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.22203v3) · [Spurious Rewards](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/shao26d.html)
### Difficulty and reward quality

Dynamic sampling and intermediate-difficulty curricula motivate a lower-cost applicability check before a large recursive-judge comparison. Our question also requires a separate balanced audit of the frozen reviewers. A grader can give varied rewards even when every solver output is incorrect. Consequently, reward variance alone cannot establish that the benchmark is solvable or that its reward is informative. [DAPO, dynamic sampling](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.14476v1#S3.SS2) · [CurES](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01037v1) · [Study amendment](learnability-preregistration.md)

## Hardware-constrained frozen reviewers

Qwen3.5 and Qwen3.8 offer open weights for a within-family deployment comparison. llama.cpp supports GGUF quantization and mixed CPU/GPU inference. Parameter count and upstream benchmark scores alone cannot determine local decoding speed or evidence-twin accuracy. The frozen-judge extension measures both on the same RTX 5070 laptop.
